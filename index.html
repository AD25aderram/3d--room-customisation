How it works

From an empty room to a furnished one in a few minutes. No account, nothing to install.

  1. Set up your room

    Pick a rectangle, square or round preset and type in the size, or draw your own outline corner by corner. Mark any pillars so you know what's in the way.

  2. Furnish it

    Add a sofa, bed, table or lamp, or import your own 3D model. Drag pieces into place, turn them, and resize them to match the real thing.

  3. Check that it fits

    Orbit around the room and zoom in to see where everything lands. Pieces can't overlap each other or sit inside walls, so with real sizes, what fits on screen is a fair test of what fits in the room.

What you can do

The basics of planning a room, without a learning curve.

  • Work in real sizes

    Rooms and furniture are measured in metres, and the editor shows your floor area as you go.

  • Draw any shape

    Trace an L-shaped room or one with an alcove, and add pillars that furniture has to go around.

  • Furniture that respects space

    Pieces can't overlap each other, the walls or the pillars, and new pieces find a free spot on their own.

  • Move, turn and resize

    Drag to move, press R or use the menu to turn a piece 90°, and type exact dimensions to match a product.

  • Bring your own models

    Import any .glb file to try a specific piece of furniture in your room.

  • Look from every angle

    Orbit, zoom and reset the view. Walls between you and the room hide automatically, so nothing blocks your view.

Who we are

We're engineering students, in the Artificial Intelligence and Software Engineering program.

We built Floorplot because buying furniture online is a guessing game. A sofa that looks right in a photo can turn out too big for the room, and the only way to find out used to be buying it. We wanted a way to check first.

Floorplot runs in your browser, so there's no account to make and nothing to download.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Open Floorplot and start planning.

Is my room saved?

Not yet. Your room is kept while you keep the browser tab open, and closing it clears the room. Saving and sharing are on our list.

Are the furniture sizes accurate?

The built-in pieces use approximate sizes. To match a real product, choose Resize on a piece and type in its measurements.

Can I use my own furniture?

Yes. Use "Import a .glb model" in the editor to add any 3D model in that format.

Does it work on a phone?

It works, but Floorplot is best on a computer. Moving and turning pieces is much easier with a mouse.
